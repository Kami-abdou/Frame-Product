How should the header stay readable over images?

Pick the contrast treatment for TopBar, logo, and borders when images sit underneath them.

FRAME

A — Top scrim

Dark gradient fades from black at the top edge down to transparent. Header floats over it. Invisible when no image, effective when image is bright.

FRAME

B — Frosted glass bar (always on)

Dark blurred bar always sits behind the header — `bg-black/60 backdrop-blur`. Clean, consistent. No gradient needed.

FRAME

C — Stronger scrim + boosted border

Heavier top gradient (85% → transparent) + frame border bumped from 4% → 25% opacity. Best for pages with very bright photos.
